## Heuristic, not authoritative

The recommendation engine combines public market data (NSE P/E zones, AMFI NAV,
RBI USD/INR), your personal ledger, and rule-based heuristics. It can be wrong.
Market data can be stale, parsers can mis-read statements, and rules can fail in
edge cases the author has not anticipated.

**Always verify Kutumb's outputs against your own bank/broker statements and
consult a SEBI-registered investment advisor before making financial decisions.**
